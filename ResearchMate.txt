# Main Problem

Students often have a research topic but struggle to:

* Find relevant research papers
* Understand papers quickly
* Compare multiple papers
* Identify methodologies used
* Find limitations
* Find future research directions
* Discover possible research gaps
* Decide which papers are relevant to cite

ResearchMate should help solve this problem.

The system should take a student from:

```text
"I have a research topic but don't know what papers to read."
```

to:

```text
"These are the important related papers,
their methods,
their limitations,
common research gaps,
and possible research directions."
```

> **How effectively can semantic embeddings identify related academic papers compared with traditional TF-IDF-based similarity methods?**
